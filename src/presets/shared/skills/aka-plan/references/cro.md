## Conversion Optimization Framework

1. Headline 4-U Formula: **Useful, Unique, Urgent, Ultra-specific** (80% won't read past this)
2. Above-Fold Value Proposition: customer problem focus, no company story, zero scroll required
3. CTA First-Person Psychology: "Get MY Guide" vs "Get YOUR Guide" (more clicks)
4. 5-Field Form Maximum: every field kills conversions; progressive profiling for the rest
5. Message Match Precision: ad copy ↔ landing page headline; broken promises = bounce
6. Social Proof Near CTAs: testimonials with faces/names + results, placed at decision points
7. Cognitive Bias Stack: loss aversion (fear), social proof (FOMO), anchoring (pricing)
8. PAS Copy Framework: Problem → Agitate → Solve; emotion before logic
9. Genuine Urgency Only: real deadlines and limits; fake timers destroy trust
10. Price Anchoring: show the expensive option first so the real price feels like relief
11. Trust Signal Clustering: security badges, guarantees, policies all visible together
12. Visual Hierarchy F-Pattern: eyes scan an F-shape — put conversions in the path
13. Lead Magnet Hierarchy: Templates > Checklists > Guides (instant > delayed gratification)
14. Objection Preemption: address top 3 concerns before they arise; FAQ near CTA
15. Mobile Thumb Zone: CTAs where thumbs naturally rest
16. One-Variable Testing: change one thing, measure, compound wins
17. Post-Conversion Momentum: thank-you page sells the next step
18. Cart Recovery Sequence: email in 1h, retarget in 4h, incentive at 24h
19. Reading Level Grade 6: short sentences, short paragraphs
20. TOFU/MOFU/BOFU Logic: match content to intent
21. White Space = Focus: empty space makes CTAs impossible to miss
22. Benefit-First Language: features tell, benefits sell, transformations compel
23. Micro-Commitment Ladder: small yes leads to big yes — start with email only
24. Performance Tracking: heatmaps (problems), recordings (why), events (what)
25. Weekly Optimization Ritual: review Monday, test Tuesday, iterate or scale
